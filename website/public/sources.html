CourtListener

Search federal and state case law, retrieve opinion text, and look up citations through Free Law Project’s authenticated API. The website’s connection is active. Local installations use your own API token.

Coverage varies by court and date. A research request reads up to three opinion records, so relevant or adverse cases may be missed. Citation lookup checks existence and ambiguity, not later treatment.

Explore CourtListener

eCFR

Search federal regulations and retrieve dated section text without a data subscription. Results identify the snapshot date. eCFR is an editorial compilation, not the official legal edition.

Explore eCFR

Federal Register

Search published rules, proposed rules, notices, and presidential documents. Results include source type and official PDF links where available. A proposal is not a final rule.

Explore the Federal Register

About the original collection

Scope and verification

Search results disclose the databases searched, retrieved passages, dates, and partial failures. Live searches read up to three full records per database and use selected excerpts for drafting. Unsupported quotations are removed; valid quotations can still be misinterpreted. Broader searches and source-by-source review remain necessary.

Student access

Free Law Project offers complimentary memberships for eligible students with an .edu address. Apply through its membership page for expanded API access. Your law library can also confirm school access to Westlaw/CoCounsel and Lexis+ AI for your own research and comparisons.

Public eCFR and Federal Register searches require no paid subscription. Provider quotas still apply. Lex Raptor does not purchase PACER records or upgrade your data plan automatically.
