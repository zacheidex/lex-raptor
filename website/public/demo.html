About coverage

CourtListener searches federal and state opinions when connected. eCFR searches regulations; the Federal Register searches published rules, proposals and notices. The offline CAP collection contains 12 Supreme Court opinions.

This is not a comprehensive search of every authority. Citation lookup does not provide KeyCite or Shepard’s treatment analysis.

Usage

Source search uses no AI credits. AI drafts share a $10 lifetime allowance, with 10 drafts per network per day. Source-service limits also apply.
